            The Natural Interface

A current fascination among application builders
where the operating system wars has spawned few
significant players with iOS, Linux, Windows, 
Android (yes I know it's also Linux), Chrome
OS (derivative of chromium) and Unix dominating
computing markets worldwide is the concept of
'natural feel'.

Reactive design, pointers & gestures, touchscreens
and the classic mouse are all examples of HCI 
standards that we have grown accustomed to. But
what about fire or chopping down a tree? Would it
be possible to recreate truly natural responses
from computers that feel more like our behaviours
inside natural environments and its pesky controls?

Certainly the world of sensors and drivers has 
evolved into sophisticated platforms for designers.
But the truly fascinating opportunity exists ins-
ide our harnessing of the force unleashed with LLMs 
like ChatGPT or Gemini. These interfaces finally 
allow natural language sentences to be parsed as if 
they were technical instructions going all the way 
down to the bare metal. But just like those adjust-
ments to your bicycle, make sure you know how to
properly test the outcome or your bare metal might
give way to bored metal.
